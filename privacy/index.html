ValuMinder — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 25 August 2026

ValuMinder is a private vault — documents, photos and videos, voice memos, notes, passwords, and a calendar — processed entirely on your device. There are no accounts, no servers we can read, no analytics, and no tracking. We are technically unable to read your content.

What the app does with your information

  • Everything you add — scanned documents, photos, videos, voice memos, notes, passwords, and calendar entries — is stored encrypted on your device (AES-256). How that key is protected depends on the unlock method you choose. With device unlock it is held in the device's Secure Enclave-backed Keychain and released only to Face ID, Touch ID or your passcode. With an app password it is not stored at all — it is derived from your password every time you unlock, so somebody holding your device without your password does not have it. With password + Face ID, both are required.
  • One exception, stated plainly: so the app can show your expiry timeline and send reminders without being unlocked, a small index stays outside that encryption — the TITLES of your documents, notes and passwords, your folder names, the holder name, the key dates, the username hint you can attach to a login, and any reference number read off a document. It is protected by iOS's own file encryption, tied to your device passcode rather than to your vault password, and it is included in your device's own backup — which is what lets a new iPhone restored from that backup find your files again. The files themselves — every scan, photo, video, recording, note and password — are always sealed with your key, in your backup as much as on your device.
  • Calendar access is full access, used only for events the app itself creates. When you tap “Add to Calendar”, ValuMinder writes one event — the document's title and its date, plus the location and what to bring if it is an appointment — into your default iOS calendar, and removes it again if you delete the document. It never reads, changes or deletes anything else in your calendar. That event leaves the vault. If your default calendar is an iCloud, Google or Exchange calendar, the event syncs wherever that calendar syncs, under that provider's protection rather than ours. It is the one thing in the app that travels in readable form, it only ever happens because you asked for it, and you can delete the event in the Calendar app at any time.
  • Documents you scan (for the Expires feature) are read on your device to recognise text and extract key dates. This uses Apple's on-device Vision and, on supported devices, Apple's on-device Foundation Models. Nothing is sent to any AI service or server for this.
  • Passwords are end-to-end local: logins, generated passwords and TOTP codes are encrypted the same way as everything else. There is no AutoFill extension and no iCloud Keychain integration — your passwords never leave your device, backed up or not.
  • Photo Library access is read, write and delete: the app reads your library to find documents you may want to import, writes to it when you save an export back to Photos, and can delete an original photo or video after you import it — only ever the specific item you chose, and only when you ask. It never touches anything else in your library.
  • Reminders are scheduled as local notifications. Their text is generic (for example “A document expires in 14 days”) so nothing sensitive shows on your lock screen.

Optional iCloud backup & sync (Plus)

Backup is scoped narrowly: only the documents in the Expires feature (passports, ID cards, permits, licences, letters and similar expiring documents) can be backed up, and only if you turn it on. Photos and videos, general documents, voice memos, notes, passwords and your calendar are always local-only and are not backed up by any plan, including Plus.

If you turn on backup for Expires documents, they are uploaded to your own private iCloud (CloudKit) storage. Before anything leaves your device it is encrypted with your key, so Apple — and we — only ever store unreadable ciphertext. The only way to restore on another device is with your recovery code, which we never receive. If you never turn this on, nothing leaves your device. Turning sync back off stops future uploads but does not delete what was already uploaded — see Deleting your data below.

What we never do

  • We never collect, sell, or share your data.
  • We use no analytics, crash-reporting, advertising or tracking SDKs. The app links only Apple's own frameworks.
  • We have no servers that can read your content.

Children

ValuMinder is rated 4+ and is not directed at children. It collects no personal data from anyone, of any age.

Subscriptions

ValuMinder Plus is an auto-renewing subscription processed by Apple. We do not receive your payment details.

Deleting your data

Delete a document in the app to remove it from your device, and from your iCloud backup if that document was ever synced. Deleting the app removes the local vault.

Turning sync off stops future uploads but does not delete Expires documents already backed up to iCloud. To remove those, go to Settings → [your name] → iCloud → ValuMinder → Delete Data from iCloud, or uninstall the app and delete its iCloud data from Settings → General → iPhone Storage.

Your rights

Because your content never reaches us, there is nothing for us to hand over, correct or erase on your behalf — you hold and delete it yourself, using the steps above. Where data-protection law (such as the UK GDPR or EU GDPR) gives you rights of access, rectification, erasure, portability or objection, they apply to personal data a controller holds about you; we hold none.

Not legal advice

ValuMinder is a document organiser and reminder utility. Extracted dates are estimates and may contain errors — always verify against your original documents and the authority that issued the document. ValuMinder does not provide immigration or legal advice.
